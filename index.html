VaxCluster - COVID-19 vaccine side-effect prediction app

My Master's degree thesis project: a full-stack web app that collects patient data and shows the probability of each side effect, along with their average. Prediction module in Python, SQL database managed through PHP, HTML/CSS/JavaScript front end.

Clustering for side-effect prediction

Compared three clustering methods (K-Means, Gaussian Mixture Models and agglomerative clustering) in Python to predict vaccine side effects, with each model's probabilities shown side by side in the app.

Patient Records — vaccination data collection app

A desktop app I built to collect patient vaccination data, now in use at EPSP Annaba. It adds, edits, searches and exports records, with form validation, a dashboard of totals and vaccine breakdowns, a medical-history checklist and dark/light mode. Records are saved to CSV files with atomic writes, so a crash can't corrupt the data.

GitHub repo ↗
PythonPyQt5CSV

COVID-19 vaccine side-effects data analysis

Exploratory analysis of 372 patient records on COVID-19 vaccine side effects, in Python. Cluster count chosen with the silhouette score, then K-Means, agglomerative and Gaussian mixture clustering, visualized with PCA and dendrograms. Each cluster is profiled by side effect, vaccine (Sputnik V, Sinovac, Sinopharm, AstraZeneca), vaccination site, pre-existing conditions and age. Fever, fatigue and muscle pain were the most frequent side effects.
